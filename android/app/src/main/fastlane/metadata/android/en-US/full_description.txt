<p>Resonance is a self-hosted shared music player for a Plex Music library.</p>

<p>Sign in to your own Resonance server, search a Music library you are
authorised to use, add requests to a shared queue, vote on requests, and take
control of playback from your Android device or Android Auto.</p>

<p>One device is the active player at a time, while everyone with access to the
selected library can browse, queue, and vote. The active player streams music
through the self-hosted Resonance server.</p>

<p>Features include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Plex PIN/forwarding sign-in</li>
<li>Shared request queue and fair automatic playback</li>
<li>Plex-backed ratings and completed-play scrobbling</li>
<li>Artist and album discovery and Track Radio</li>
<li>Android Auto search, queue, playback, and voting controls</li>
</ul>

<p>Resonance has no central service, advertising, analytics, or telemetry. It
communicates with the server URL you configure, which in turn uses Plex to
access the selected Music library.</p>

<p>Resonance is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Plex.</p>
